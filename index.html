Submission guidelines

What counts as useful criticism?

GOOD 01

Specific.

“You interrupt when you get excited and it makes people feel unheard” is useful. “Bad vibes” is harder to debug.

GOOD 02

Something he can act on.

A behavior, habit, blind spot, presentation issue, interpersonal pattern, bad argument, weak piece of writing, or questionable haircut decision.

GOOD 03

Uncomfortably true.

If your first instinct is “he probably does not want to hear this,” that may be precisely why this domain exists.

Terms and conditions

Cooper agrees to the following.

01

No arguing with the feedback.

He may privately think you are wrong. He is not entitled to convene a debate panel.

02

No fishing for compliments.

“Actually you are great” is not the intended output of this system, although legally it will be accepted.

03

No pretending every criticism is profound.

Sometimes the lesson really is just “stop doing that annoying thing.” Humility includes not turning every note into a philosophy seminar.

The complaint department

Say the thing.

Privacy note: this is a static website. Clicking send opens your own email app with the text filled in. The website itself does not store your submission.
FAQ

Before you destroy him.

Will Cooper be offended?

Possibly. The goal is not to become chemically incapable of offense. The goal is to hear useful things anyway.

Can I be anonymous?

Yes. Leave the name blank. Your email address may still appear because this uses your email client.

Can I compliment him?

Technically, but please understand that you are undermining the entire business model.

What if my criticism is “this website is obnoxious”?

Accepted. Ironically, that would validate the website.
